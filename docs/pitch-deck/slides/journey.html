One thread through five moments

Before you join

An honest vetting tracker, kit and benefit choices, and meeting your manager and buddy, so the wait is never silent.

Day 1

One plan: who meets you, where you sit and how to get there. One sign-in opens everything that is ready.

Week 1

Guided prompts for stakeholder intros, mandatory training and your first 1:1 with your manager.

Day 30

A two-question check-in, “I know who to ask” and “I feel confident in my role”, then a celebration.

Day 90

First three-month goals set by your manager before you start, and buddy meetups tracked towards four to eight or more.

This is the heart of the pitch: the same app, the same thread, from before you join to Day 90. Before joining, the vetting tracker works like parcel tracking, with plain-English status and honest timings, and the joiner makes real choices such as laptop and benefits, and meets their manager and buddy. On Day 1 they get one plan rather than a pile of emails. In week 1 the app prompts the intros, training and first 1:1. At Day 30 a two-question pulse asks whether they know who to ask and whether they feel confident, and the manager sees the trend, never individual answers. Day 90 is where the journey points: goals agreed before Day 1, and a buddy tracker measured against Microsoft’s finding that 4 to 8 or more meetups in 90 days made the buddy genuinely useful. Today’s build is fully worked through Day 30, with Day 90 carried by goals, the buddy tracker and the handover to the team.
