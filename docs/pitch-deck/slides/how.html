How it works, and why we designed it this way

How it works

One engine, four views

Joiner, manager, buddy and people team read the same journey.

Everything is an event

Steps unlock from checks, dates and role, so changes ripple through.

Chain reactions

A manager sets goals and the joiner’s welcome pack appears.

An assistant that knows its limits

Cites policy, knows where you are, then hands over to a person.

Design choices

One next step, not a to-do list

A single hero card shows the most useful action.

Locked means earned

Every locked step says what unlocks it, so nothing feels withheld.

Honest about the wait

Real timings, never sensitive results.

Built for change

Slip the start date and the journey, tasks and messages all move.

Left side, how it works: one journey engine drives the joiner, manager, buddy and people-team views, so they are never out of step. Everything is an event, such as a check clearing, a laptop chosen or a goal set, and unlocks are rules over those events plus the joiner’s role, location and software. That gives chain reactions: when the manager sets goals, the welcome pack lands in the joiner’s app live. The assistant answers from policy with citations, knows the joiner’s own steps, and offers a person with a short summary when it is unsure or the topic is sensitive, such as pay. Right side, why: we show one next step because a 40-item checklist is overwhelming; locked items say what unlocks them so waiting feels earned; vetting is shown honestly with realistic timings but never results; and because start dates move in real life, the whole journey reflows when one slips. It is also privacy-minded: managers and buddies see signals and trends, never private chats.
