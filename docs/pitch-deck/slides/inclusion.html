Inclusion isn’t a separate page, it is part of the journey. Before Day 1 every joiner is asked, warmly and with three options (yes, not right now, or I’d prefer to talk to someone first), whether they need any adjustments. It is confidential: details go to IT and Occupational Health, and the manager only learns that a request was made. The answer also drives the journey, because laptop and headset choices wait for it, so the right equipment is ready on Day 1 rather than sorted weeks later. A jargon buster removes the barrier of unexplained banking acronyms. And because we built six very different personas, including a contact-centre advisor, branch colleague, graduate and a remote analyst, no one is handed a head-office template. The app is mobile-first with no extra login, and we designed for contrast, large touch targets and reduced motion. Close on the aim: connected and confident, from the first yes to Day 90.
