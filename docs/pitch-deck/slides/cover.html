Open with the aim, not the features. Joining a bank takes weeks of vetting, and today the experience is a series of separate handovers: recruiter, vetting team, IT, manager, HR. Our idea is that the joiner should feel one continuous journey, from the moment they say yes to the point, around three months in, where they are part of the team. The app is a mobile-first companion for the joiner, with matching views for their manager and buddy. Everything you will see is built and running on a phone; all people and policies are fictional.
