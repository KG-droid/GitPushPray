# Market research: preboarding and onboarding (offer to Day 30)

How leading HR-tech products and companies handle the period from accepted offer to the first 30 days, and what we can borrow for an LBG new-joiner companion. Compiled 9 Nov 2026.

Most sources are vendor pages or HR blogs, so treat vendor statistics as marketing. The strongest evidence is the Microsoft buddy study, Google's nudge result and Speer (2026, peer-reviewed).

## 1. Table stakes

- **Journeys started by HR systems.** A new hire record starts a sequence of tasks, forms, e-signatures and content from offer to Day 90. Examples: Workday Journeys, ServiceNow Employee Journeys, Enboarder, Talmundo.
- **Personalised by role, department, location and employment type.** Rippling's conditional logic gives each role group access to its apps automatically.
- **Separate manager and buddy roles**, each with their own tasks, check-ins and conversation starters (Enboarder, Talmundo).
- **Mobile-first preboarding with little or no login.** Enboarder reaches frontline staff by SMS and WhatsApp.
- **Social profiles and a people finder.** HiBob has photo and video intros and interest clubs. Appical has a People Finder and a jargon glossary.
- **Pulse surveys and onboarding analytics** (Leapsome, Rippling, HiBob).
- **AI assistants for new-hire questions** (Bob AI, Rippling AI, ServiceNow Now Assist). ServiceNow hands the chat to a live HR agent when needed.

## 2. Standout ideas worth borrowing

- **Google's just-in-time manager nudge.** An email the Sunday before the start date lists 5 actions: discuss role, assign a buddy, help build a network, check in monthly for 6 months, encourage open dialogue. Hires whose managers acted on it were fully effective about 25% faster. This compares managers who acted with those who didn't; it was not a randomised test.
- **Microsoft's buddy result.** The more often someone met their buddy in their first 90 days, the more they said the buddy helped: 56% after 1 meeting, 73% after 2–3, 86% after 4–8 and 97% after more than 8. These figures are self-reported.
- **Appical's countdown and phased unlocking.** A Day-1 countdown, preboarding content unlocking 10 days before the start, points and "Insider" status.
- **Enboarder's journeys driven by behaviour.** Nudges fire when someone does, or fails to do, something, not on fixed dates.
- **Monzo's protected diary.** An onboarding week with diaries kept free, week 2 for building relationships, a "first microservice" project for engineers.
- **Netflix:** culture deck in week one, real ownership early, equipment ready before Day 1.

## 3. UX patterns that feel modern

- A vertical timeline with phases: Offer, Vetting, Pre-start, Day 1, Week 1, Day 30.
- A Day-1 countdown and content that unlocks in stages.
- A "Today" card showing one next action, not a 40-item checklist.
- A chat-first assistant with cited sources and a visible "talk to a human" option.
- Nudges through whatever channel the person uses (push, SMS, Teams).
- Light gamification: missions and badges, no leaderboards.
- Short video intros from the manager and team.

## 4. Pitfalls to avoid

- **Silence during the gap before Day 1.** Each extra week between accepted offer and start raised the odds of the hire never showing up by about 22% (Speer, 2026). Click Boarding found 45% of new-hire ghosting happens between offer and Day 1, and 41% of UK candidates say poor communication makes them less committed to an accepted offer.
- **Unlocking confidential content before checks clear.** Regulatory references under SM&CR cover up to 6 years, and a firm should respond within 6 weeks (SYSC 22). Credit checks are tied to the FCA "fit and proper" test (FIT 2.1). BPSS covers ID, right to work, a Basic DBS check and 3 years of employment history. Show status honestly, never the sensitive results.
- **Treating start dates as fixed.** When a date slips, automated messages fire at the wrong time.
- **Checklist overload**, and points that make compliance feel trivial.
- **An AI assistant that answers HR or policy questions confidently without grounding**, with no escalation route.
- **Measuring task completion instead of connection and confidence.**

## 5. Feature ideas that would stand out in our demo

1. **Vetting tracker that keeps people engaged.** A parcel-tracking-style timeline: ID, Right to work, Criminal record, Credit, Regulatory reference, Cleared. Plain-English stages and realistic time estimates, "what you can do to speed things up", and non-confidential culture content to fill the wait. Gated content unlocks automatically once cleared.
2. **Journey that reflows when the start date slips.** Recalculates unlocks, nudges and manager tasks relative to the new Day 1, sends an empathetic message, tells the manager and buddy, and flags people at risk of dropping out.
3. **Manager nudge engine and buddy meeting tracker.** Just-in-time cards for managers with Google's 5 actions, and a buddy view that tracks meetings against the Microsoft thresholds.
4. **Policy-grounded assistant with a warm handover to a human.** Cites the policy passage it used. Hands over to HR or the buddy, with a conversation summary, when it isn't confident or the topic is sensitive (pay, adjustments, a vetting problem).
5. **Day-30 "Connected and Confident" score.** A two-question weekly pulse plus signals such as buddy meetings and introductions made. Managers see a trend line with suggested actions.
